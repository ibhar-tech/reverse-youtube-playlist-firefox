# Privacy Policy for YouTube Playlist Tools

**Effective Date:** August 2, 2026

**YouTube Playlist Tools** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection
YouTube Playlist Tools does **NOT** collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or user information. 

- **No Analytics & No Telemetry:** We do not track your usage or collect diagnostic data.
- **No Third-Party Requests:** The Extension does not communicate with external servers, Google, YouTube APIs, or third-party tracking services.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to sign in or connect any account to use the Extension.

---

## 2. Local Storage Only
All data created by the Extension—such as saved playlist snapshots, custom video orders, watched video status, and user preferences—is stored **100% locally** within your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. 

- Your saved playlists and settings remain on your physical device and are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud storage.
- You can clear your data at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser storage.

---

## 3. Permissions Used
The Extension requests the minimum permissions necessary for its single purpose:

- **`storage`**: Allows the Extension to save your custom playlist snapshots, watched video badges, and preferences locally on your device.
- **`activeTab`**: Allows the Extension to interact with the active YouTube tab to modify playlist playback sequence.
- **Host Permission (`*://*.youtube.com/*`)**: Enables the content script to run on YouTube watch pages with playlists (`?list=`) to display the toolbar and panel controls.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not share, sell, trade, or transfer any user information to outside parties because no user data is collected in the first place.
